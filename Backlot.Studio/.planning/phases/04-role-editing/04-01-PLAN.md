<objective>
Stand up the schema-driven role edit page (`/roles/{uid}/edit`) and the service/DTO layer that backs it, and front-load the phase's one unverified hazard: that a Razor Pages Turbo-driven form emits a 303 on success and a 422 on invalid, surviving a prior Turbo navigation, against the RUNNING Backlot API.

This plan delivers EDIT-01: a form with all editable fields rendered from the `director/roles` schema, pre-filled from `seekbase/detail`. It also wires a minimal but REAL save handler (isvalid -> 422 / persist -> 303) so the Turbo contract can be smoke-tested early. Plan 04-02 then hardens the validation summary, mass-assignment-safe payload building, and the success banner.

Purpose: Isolate and prove the Turbo 303/422 + antiforgery hazard (STATE.md blocker, RESEARCH A4) before building out the full validation surface, and deliver the editable-field rendering required by EDIT-01.
Output: TurboEditPageModel base, three new BacklotApiClient methods, RoleSchema/ValidationOutcome DTOs, and the Edit page (GET render + minimal POST round-trip), smoke-tested against the live API.
</objective>
